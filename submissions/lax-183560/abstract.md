This draft extends an overview and hierarchy of graph parameters by defining
five width parameters:

- treedepth;
- pathwidth;
- branchwidth;
- rank-width;
- modular-width.

Throughout, graphs are finite, simple, and undirected, and may be disconnected.
All five parameters assign zero to the empty graph. Treedepth counts vertices
in the height of a rooted forest. Branchwidth is zero for graphs with at most
one edge, and modular-width is zero for cographs.

The draft also defines the underlying structures:

- rooted forests;
- path decompositions;
- branch decompositions;
- cut-rank;
- modular decompositions.
